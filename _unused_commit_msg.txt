fix(public): block page-level horizontal scroll on mobile

User reported the home page was horizontally scrolled on mobile/tablet,
with content like "Daftar Lomba Kp. Kadu" cut off on the LEFT side
(only "Kadu" visible). Page was wider than viewport.

**Root cause:**
The chip strip on the home page uses:
- `-mx-4 px-4` to bleed to viewport edges
- `min-w-max` on inner div for natural chip width
- `overflow-x: auto` on the outer for internal scroll

In some browser/viewport combinations, the `min-w-max` inner
div can push the outer's effective width beyond the app-content
boundary, which then pushes the body to scroll horizontally.

The `overflow-x: auto` on the chip outer is supposed to contain
this, but on certain tablet widths (around 768-900px), the
auto scroll context doesn't fully prevent the body from
scrolling.

**Fix (globals.css):**

1. Added `overflow-x: hidden` to `.mobile-page`:
   Prevents the body from scrolling horizontally regardless of
   what child tries to overflow. Chip strip's own `overflow-x: auto`
   still works (it creates its own scroll context within the
   hidden parent).

2. Added `overflow-x: hidden` to `.app-content`:
   Belt-and-suspenders — the app-content is where the search bar,
   filter chips, and card grid live. Clipping here catches any
   future overflow.

3. Did NOT add `overflow-x: hidden` to body:
   The sticky app-header has a box-shadow that extends below it.
   Body-level `overflow: hidden` would clip the shadow, making
   the header look flat. Keeping it on mobile-page (which is BELOW
   the header) preserves the shadow.

**No JSX or layout changes.** Pure CSS defensive fix.
